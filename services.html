Our Services

Everything Royal Rides offers is built around one thing: getting employees between home and work safely, comfortably and on schedule.

Employee Transport

Our core service. We collect employees from their pickup area in Phoenix and bring them to their workplace, then take them home again after their shift. Journeys are arranged around the shift you work, not a fixed public timetable.

  • Home-to-work and work-to-home travel
  • Pickup points agreed with you before the first trip
  • Comfortable seating in a 7-seater Renault Triber

Staff Shuttle Services

Shuttle transport for groups of staff travelling the same route. Useful where several colleagues live near each other in Phoenix and work at the same destination, and would rather travel together than arrange separate transport.

  • Shared travel for colleagues on the same route
  • One agreed pickup point per group where possible
  • Arranged directly with you over the phone or WhatsApp

Night Shift Transport

Flexible transport for employees who work after hours. Night shift travel needs to be dependable, so we agree your pickup point and route with you beforehand rather than leaving it to chance on the night.

  • Transport for employees working night shifts
  • Pickup points confirmed in advance
  • Same comfortable vehicle as our daytime runs

Scheduled Runs

Regular, repeating trips for employees who travel the same route every working day. Once your route and shift are agreed, the trip simply runs, so you are not rebooking every morning.

  • Flexible scheduled runs across morning, day and night shifts
  • Route agreed once, then repeated
  • Changes arranged by contacting Neren or Melaine

Local Transport

Local trips connecting Phoenix with surrounding areas and major destinations, including Umhlanga, Cornubia Mall, Oceans Mall, La Lucia Mall, Mount Edgecombe and Durban North.

  • Travel between Phoenix and surrounding destinations
  • Comfortable seating for up to 7 passengers
  • Arranged on request through phone or WhatsApp
