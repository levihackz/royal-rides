About Royal Rides

Safe and reliable local transport and shuttle services connecting Phoenix to surrounding areas, with employee and work transport at the centre of what we do.

Who We Are

Transport built around the working day

Getting to work should be the easiest part of the day. Royal Rides exists to take that worry off employees in Phoenix, by providing comfortable transport between the areas they live in and the places they work.

We keep it simple: agree your pickup point, agree your shift, and travel in a vehicle that is comfortable enough to relax in before and after a long day.
